## Constructor, replay and uncertain durability

Constructor replay validates deterministic closed headers, histories, keys, predecessor facts, receipt associations and binding digests without granting current-owner authority or writing anything. For this opt-in mode, structural benchmark completion is accepted only if its required receipts/bindings and finished protocol facts already occur in the replay prefix. A completion followed by a later binding is invalid, even if final arrays look complete. Existing core/runtime reducer division remains explicit; do not call the runtime reducer's protocol no-op an authority decision.

Current-owner locked refresh must replay in order, freshly reverify reports/source/evidence, recompute comparisons and measured gates, and only then set authority-ready state. A changed file, forged receipt, wrong binding, owner/source mismatch or failed gate prevents queries/mutations from treating the recorded completion as current authority. Constructor receipts remain provisional. Existing modes that do not opt in must not begin accepting new benchmark completion merely because its event is well formed.

Keep the same lock, native liveness and explicit acquisition/recovery policy. No automatic takeover, unlock, abandonment, retry, reset, root replacement, history rewrite or reservation rollback. Unknown/malformed liveness retains the lock. A poisoned/uncertain instance cannot be revived by a caught exception, later key, healthy-looking artifact or repeated completion call.

Native effect-then-throw cuts can leave actual admitted receipts, bindings or `stage_completed` bytes visible despite public rejection. Preserve that actual prefix and retained lock; no promise that every failed call leaves completion absent. After explicit permitted ownership/recovery, a fresh instance uses existing locked replay to determine whether old state, uncertain publication, or fully retained completion exists. It must reverify authority rather than infer success from a filename or duplicate caller request. Idempotence may avoid duplicate events only after fresh verification; it is never a same-instance retry after poison.
