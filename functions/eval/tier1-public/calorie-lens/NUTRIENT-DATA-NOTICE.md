# Nutrition5k nutrient metadata

Original, unmodified ingredient metadata by Thames et al., *Nutrition5k*, CVPR 2021, Google Research. Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/nutrition5k_dataset/nutrition5k_dataset/metadata/ingredients_metadata.csv . Dataset repository: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Nutrition5k . Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Attribution and source version are retained in the experiment profile and per-record provenance. The CSV bytes, including CRLF, are unchanged (SHA256 87a9bbd1fd2e28f09f16305a04946ddbe31eb7d9d03686d7e2fb3d8f19f3effb).

The model config contains publisher-derived float32 densities and additional USDA-match claims. An exact source-table match does not verify a model's predicted food identity or grams. Extended USDA fields and the average-training-plate mass-gap prior are not independently verified USDA record matches.
